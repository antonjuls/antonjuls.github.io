# EarthRay — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 22, 2026

## Who We Are

EarthRay is developed by Anton Skrynnik (antonjuls), an independent developer based in the Netherlands.

## Data We Collect and Why

| Data                                | Purpose                                                     | Storage                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Precise location (GPS)              | Core app functionality — ray calculation, globe positioning | **Used locally only.** Location is fuzzed by ~30 km before being sent to the relay server or other users. Precise coordinates are never transmitted or stored. |
| Device orientation (motion sensors) | Determines phone pointing direction for AR ray              | Real-time local processing only, never stored or transmitted                                                                                                   |
| Camera feed                         | AR overlay display                                          | Local display only — never recorded, transmitted, or stored                                                                                                    |
| Display name (user-chosen)          | Shown to other session participants                         | Relay server, in-memory only, active session — auto-deleted on disconnect                                                                                      |
| Crash and error reports             | Finding and fixing crashes and bugs                         | Sent to Sentry (EU region). See [Crash and Error Reports](#crash-and-error-reports)                                                                            |
| Anonymous usage statistics          | Understanding which features are used and where people get stuck | Sent to Aptabase (EU region), no identifier. Can be turned off in Settings. See [Usage Statistics](#usage-statistics)                                     |

## Authentication

EarthRay uses anonymous session-only authentication. On joining a room, the relay server issues a random anonymous user ID and a short-lived session token (self-issued JWT, 1-hour expiry). No email, password, phone number, or real identity is collected. The anonymous ID and token are held only in device memory during the session and are not persisted after the session ends.

## Crash and Error Reports

When the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, it sends a report to Sentry so the developer can find and fix the problem. A report contains:

- the error message and stack trace (which part of the app's code failed);
- the app version, device model, operating system version, language and free memory;
- a short trail of preceding app events (for example, "opened the Globe tab", "connection to the relay lost");
- a random installation identifier generated by the crash reporter, used only to count how many installs a problem affects.

Reports are filtered on the device before they are sent: location, coordinates, ray data, room codes and display names are removed. Reports do not include your IP address, name, email, contacts or any account. No screen recording or session replay is used.

## Usage Statistics

The app sends anonymous usage events to Aptabase, for example "app opened", "room joined" or "tab viewed", together with the app version, operating system and language. Events carry only fixed values (such as which tab or which error code), never location, room codes, names or free text.

There is no persistent identifier: events are grouped only into a temporary session that exists in device memory and ends after a period of inactivity. Your IP address is not stored. The statistics cannot be used to identify you or to follow you across apps or websites.

You can turn usage statistics off at any time in **Settings → Anonymous statistics**. When it is off, no usage events are sent.

## Feature Rollouts

Some features may be switched on gradually, for a share of installs at a time, through the app configuration served by the relay server. To decide whether a feature is on for your install, the app creates a random install identifier and keeps it in local device storage. This identifier is used only on the device; it is never sent to the relay server or to anyone else.

## Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

- **Consent** (Art. 6(1)(a)) — Location access requires explicit device permission before any data is processed.
- **Performance of a contract** (Art. 6(1)(b)) — Relaying your chosen display name and ray state to the other participants of a session you joined is necessary to provide the multiplayer experience you requested.
- **Legitimate interest** (Art. 6(1)(f)) — Processing device orientation and other session data is necessary for core app functionality (real-time AR ray visualization), and for protecting the relay server against abuse (rate limiting, anti-flooding). Crash and error reports are necessary to keep the app working and secure. Anonymous usage statistics help improve the app; they contain no identifier and you can turn them off in Settings.

## What We Do NOT Collect

No advertising or tracking SDKs. No advertising identifiers (IDFA / Android Advertising ID). No tracking across apps or websites. No profiling. No cookies. No contacts. No photos. No health data. No browsing history. No purchase data. No Google or Firebase services.

## Third-Party Services

- **Cloud hosting provider** — used to host the EarthRay relay server, which synchronizes session state between participants in real time. The relay holds session data only in memory; it has no database, does not log user state, and does not retain anything after disconnect. Depending on the deployment region, session data may be processed in countries outside the European Economic Area. Where this is the case, transfers are protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs).
- **Sentry** (Functional Software, Inc.) — crash and error reporting, data stored in Sentry's EU region (Germany). Sentry acts as a processor under a data processing agreement. As a US company, it may access data from outside the EEA; such transfers are protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and SCCs.
- **Aptabase** — anonymous usage statistics, data stored in Aptabase's EU region. Aptabase acts as a processor and receives no identifier that links events to a person or device.

## Data Sharing

Fuzzed location and ray data are shared only with users in the same session (requires a room code) and with the cloud hosting provider of the relay server. Crash reports and anonymous usage statistics are shared only with the processors listed above, solely to provide those services to the developer. No data is sold to third parties. No advertising. No profiling.

## Data Retention

The relay server holds session data only in memory for the duration of an active session. On disconnect, the server removes the user from the room after a brief grace period (~10 seconds, to tolerate momentary network blips) and broadcasts the departure to remaining participants. When the last user leaves a room, the room itself is deleted from memory after ~30 seconds of idleness. No data is written to disk, no database is used, and server restarts clear all state. There are no persistent user accounts.

Crash and error reports are kept in Sentry for up to 90 days and then deleted automatically. Anonymous usage statistics are kept in Aptabase as aggregated, non-identifying event records.

Local device storage (AsyncStorage) holds only app preferences — your chosen display name, the last room code you used, a boolean flag indicating whether you were previously connected (used to restore the join screen on reopen), the current remote-config snapshot served by the relay, your usage-statistics setting, and the random install identifier used for feature rollouts. The crash reporter may keep a pending report on the device until it can be sent.

## Children

EarthRay is intended for general audiences. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Children may use the app under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian, who is responsible for ensuring use is appropriate.

## Your Rights (GDPR)

The developer is based in the EU (Netherlands). Under GDPR, you have the right to:

- Access, correct, or delete your personal data
- Restrict or object to processing — for usage statistics, simply turn them off in Settings
- Lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) at [autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl](https://autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl)

In practice, all session data is already auto-deleted on disconnect, and crash reports and usage statistics contain no information that identifies you, so they usually cannot be linked back to a specific person. For any requests, contact the developer.

## International Users

EarthRay is available worldwide. The data practices described above apply to all users regardless of location. Specifically:

- **EU / EEA residents** have the GDPR rights listed above. The Dutch DPA (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) is the lead supervisory authority.
- **UK residents** have equivalent rights under the UK GDPR; the supervisory authority is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).
- **California residents** have rights under the CCPA / CPRA, including the right to know, delete, and opt out of "sales" of personal information. EarthRay does not sell personal information, does not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and does not use sensitive personal information for inferring characteristics.
- **Other jurisdictions** — where local data protection law grants additional rights, those rights are honored. Contact the developer to exercise them.
